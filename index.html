Before you install

  • Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.
  • Internet for the AI, voice and live data.
  • An AI key. Groq has a free tier.
  • Python is not needed. It is bundled.

Install in five steps

  1. Run Aegis-Setup.exe and choose a folder.
  2. Open Aegis. It starts its own server on this PC only.
  3. Create an account. It is stored locally.
  4. Settings > AI provider: paste the key, Test, Save.
  5. Say “Hey Aegis” and talk.

Questions people ask first

Can Aegis delete my files without me knowing?

No. Writing, moving, deleting, typing and shell commands all show an Allow or Deny prompt first. If you decline, or do not answer, Aegis does not retry that action.

What do I need for voice and meeting notes?

A free Groq key for fast Whisper transcription, added in Settings. Without it, voice falls back to your browser’s own speech recognition. Meeting notes need the key, and your audio is sent only to the service you choose.

What does it remember about me?

Only facts you share or ask it to save. They appear in the memory panel and you can delete any of them at any time.

Does it work on Mac or Linux?

The web app works in any browser. Opening apps, typing and key presses on your computer are Windows-only for now.

Can it speak my language?

It replies in the language and script you write in, including Roman Urdu, Urdu, Hindi, Arabic, Spanish and French. Optional local voices cover 31 languages plus Roman Urdu with no per-character cost.
